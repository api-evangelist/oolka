Your credit score does not drop without a reason. A reported delay, high credit utilisation, a new loan enquiry, corrected lender data, or an error in your credit report is almost always responsible for the change. Akhil, 37, from Guwahati, logged in to check his score before applying for a ₹10 lakh personal loan.
